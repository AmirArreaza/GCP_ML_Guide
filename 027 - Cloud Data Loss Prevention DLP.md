Cloud DLP (Data Loss Prevention), now part of **Sensitive Data Protection**, is a fully managed service designed to help you discover, classify, and protect your most sensitive data. 🛡️ Think of it as an automated security guard that scans your files and databases to find and mask information that shouldn't be exposed.

Let's explore this together! I'll ask guiding questions along the way so we can break down how it works.

To help us get started, here is a quick look at its core capabilities:

* **🔍 Discovery & Classification:** Automatically identifying sensitive data—like credit card numbers, social security numbers, names, and phone numbers—across your storage buckets, databases, or even raw text.
* **🎭 De-identification:** Masking, encrypting, or replacing that sensitive data so it can be safely used for analysis or training without exposing real information.

2. **How it protects data:** Learning about de-identification techniques like masking, tokenization, and bucketing.
